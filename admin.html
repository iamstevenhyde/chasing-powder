End-of-Game Debrief

0. The AI Clockspeed Shift
The season broad AI access landed on every resort at once, and what it did to the rate at which a held position goes stale. Every team saw this named in its own season log the season it happened, and this is the room-level version of that same event.
1. Stage-Read Scorecard
Each team's guessed industry stage against the hidden true stage, season by season. The true stage is derived from the game's own segment lifecycle, not a fixed season number, so a fast- or slow-moving world gets its own read. This was never shown to teams during play.
2. Intended vs Realized Movement
Where a team aimed its levers each season versus where it actually landed, summed across the game. Movement lands short of intent when the market it is chasing is thin, or when rivals are already crowding the same ground; it never lands past intent. A big shortfall is the imperfect-movement lesson made visible: aiming does not guarantee arriving.
3. Final Standings
Score is the engine's own terminal measure: this season's profit plus the value of the position and reputation held at the end. Vail and Alta are shown for comparison, tagged as the permanent AI rivals they are.

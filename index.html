Your turn. Every season: spend a budget across four levers, set two prices, and set capacity. You never move your position directly. You spend, and the market decides how far you actually move based on nearby demand and rival crowding.

What to watch.

  • Track-out: standing still still costs you. It gets worse after the market's turning point.
  • Gates: Lodging caps your Comfort score until you've spent past a threshold. Once open, it stays open.
  • Moat rings: sit still long enough and rings grow around your sphere. Two-sided bets grow them faster.
  • Lifecycle signals: the Patrol Report gives a fuzzy heads-up (a range, never an exact number) when a segment is about to shift.
  • Rival moves: the AI resorts run scripted competitive plays against each other and you; the season log narrates what hit you. You answer through position, price, and capacity.
  • Two scores at the end: an official cash ranking, and a diagnostic consistency score. Neither is favored; that call isn't made yet.
